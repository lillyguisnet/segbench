Controls & saving

Wheel: zoom at cursor. Space + drag: pan. F: fit. N: next unchecked. U: least sure unchecked. P: previous. Enter: keep & next. Delete: remove & next. A: draw a box. V: select. Arrows: move the box's edge under the last handle you used, or the whole box. Ctrl/Cmd Z: undo.

Autosave is in this browser only. Export the JSON to keep or move your work; Import restores it.
