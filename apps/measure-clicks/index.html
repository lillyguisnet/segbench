    Controls
    
   Drafts (orange, dashed) come from GPT-6 Astra and SAM 3: fix their points and dot, then Enter keeps one and goes to the next; Delete removes a wrong or unclear one. Only kept items count.
    
   Click to add a point. A span ends by itself after 2 clicks; a line ends with Enter (or a double-click).
   Backspace removes the last point while clicking. Esc cancels.
    
   Drag an empty spot to pan; wheel to zoom at the cursor; F to fit.
   Drag a point or the red dot to move it.
    
   The red dot is what the models will see, at the size they will see it. It goes to the middle of your clicks
   (half-way along a line); drag it if it is not clearly on the part.
    
   N / P: next / previous question. Ctrl/Cmd Z: undo.
