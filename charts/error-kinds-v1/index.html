Correct boxes and the four kinds of mistakes (square)

"Correct" here means the box overlaps a real object's box by at least half (the loosest of the four levels). The score also checks 75%, 90% and 95%, so a model with many loose boxes can be high here and lower in the score (GPT-6 Astra: 86% correct, score 57; Gemini 4 Argon: 84%, score 63). svg · pdf
