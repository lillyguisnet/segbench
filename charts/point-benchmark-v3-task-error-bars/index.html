Dot: each model's best run: of its complete passes over the 11 tasks (3 for language models, 1 for local models), the one with the highest score. On the ranked chart, the black tick is the mean of its runs (the score on the main chart).

Bar: how far that score could move with a different set of tasks of the same kind. It comes from how much the model's 11 task scores differ from each other (Student-t interval), widened so all bars on a chart together hold with 95% confidence (Bonferroni: 28 models, or 19 language models only). Cut at 0% and 100%.

Why the bars are so long: the tasks differ a lot in difficulty (nearly every model finds the white cow; no model gets above 61% on the pink leaves), and 11 tasks is a small sample. That difficulty is the same for every model, so overlapping bars do not mean two models are tied: compared task by task, the top models' differences are known to within about ±2 to ±5 points. Even so, with a strict correction over all 378 pairs of models, only 17 pairs are clearly different.

Not in the bars: luck between tries (the best run is taken as it is). Picking the best run on these same photos flatters the language models, which had 3 runs, and the erratic ones most: GLM 5.3 Flash gains 10 points, Mistral Large 4 seven. The tasks were chosen by us, not drawn at random, so "other tasks of the same kind" is an idealisation. Cost and seconds are as on the main chart.
